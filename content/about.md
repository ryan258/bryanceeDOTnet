Bryan grew up in The Bahamas listening to classical music and playing in church. In his notes for “Passacaglia Variations,” he remembers the deal his first piano teacher made with him. Carl Johnson would teach him if he played for church. Bryan went on to spend more than a decade as organist and music director at his parish, study piano, organ, and tuba at St. John’s University, and earn a master’s in piano performance at McNally Smith College of Music.

His work has been featured by the Bahamas Ministry of Tourism and the Bahamas Ministry of Education. He has performed at Caribbean carnival events in The Bahamas, Atlanta, and Minnesota, and at Midwest events including the Global Market Festival, Winter Carnival, and St. Paul’s Riverfront Prototype Festival.

Bryan released “Long Lost Brother” with a music video in 2012. The song later appeared on his debut, *The Wibe of Life*. For *Diary of a Pandemic*, he drew on life at home and on the trails of nearby nature reserves. In the liner notes, he writes about everything from the weight of isolation to the joy of getting invited to tea by Princess Fairy Zoe.

His work as a teacher spans kindergarten through college. He has coached singers, taught piano, directed choirs and bands, and started a youth orchestra. In Chicago, he has worked as a theater pit musician and as a music director and accompanist with Chicago Public Schools through the Professional Dance Theater Youth Academy Company.
